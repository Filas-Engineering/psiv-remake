# Feature checklist from published player guides

What the original game does, as players describe it in published guides, written here as rules and features in this repo's own words. No names, numbers tables, text or story from the original are recorded. Each line is something to check against the libraries and the sample: built, partly built, or missing.

The status column is filled in by an audit against the code: `built` names what exercises it (a test, an acceptance check or a headless script), `partly` says what is missing, and `missing` has nothing. Where the research documents or the spec disagree with a row, they win and the row says so.
