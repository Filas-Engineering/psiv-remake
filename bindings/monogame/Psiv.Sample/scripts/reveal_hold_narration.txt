# The dialogue layout's `reveal_confirm` "hold" in narration (make_options_assets.py --hold over the narration assets,
# make_narration_data.py), with --dev: a press does not complete a narration line, holding Confirm reveals it at
# `reveal_hold_cps` 200 in place of the 40 a second of `narration_cps`, and a shown line goes on with a press; the
# `dialogue_open_sound` plays when the box opens for the guide's line after the narration, not for narration, which has
# no box.

expect screen Title
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 800
assets swap ui/dialogue.json alt/dialogue_hold.json
wait 2
cmd set text normal
audio mark
dev event narration
waitstate 10 narrationreveal 0/40
wait 26
expect state narrationreveal 8/40
# A press is one frame held: three characters more at 200 a second, the line not completed.
press Enter
expect state narrationreveal 11/40
# Held: 200 a second, five times the line's own pace; 40 characters in 12 frames of 16 ms.
hold Enter 4
expect statenot narrationreveal 40/40
hold Enter 8
expect state narrationreveal 40/40
expect audiocount 0 cue brass_alarm
# The other four lines each held to their end and gone on from; then the guide's line opens the box.
press Enter
hold Enter 20
press Enter
hold Enter 20
press Enter
hold Enter 20
press Enter
hold Enter 20
expect state narration Then the fog lifted, all at once.
expect audiocount 0 cue brass_alarm
# The last line goes on to the guide's, which waits under the narration for a press (narration_wait).
press Enter
wait 2
expect audiocount 0 cue brass_alarm
press Enter
waitstate 10 speaker Harbor Guide
expect audiocount 1 cue brass_alarm
pressuntil Enter Field 60
