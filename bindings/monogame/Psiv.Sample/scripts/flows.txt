# The layout flows (make_flows_assets.py) on the front data copy, with --dev: the title's intro on boot and its start
# state; the field menu's entries as a list (`navigation` "list"): Left and Right doing nothing, Up and Down choosing,
# the page shown only once confirmed, Back from the page to the list and from the list closing the menu, a tab's children,
# a page Extras opens and the System action open at once; the Items page one row per copy with the count drawn apart, and
# stacked again; the shop's flow (welcome, mode, list, a YES/NO confirm of one, result, farewell); the inn's flow
# (welcome, price, choice, result over the field after the rest, farewell); travel with one destination going at once,
# the destinations as plain names without a Cancel row, and the messages before leaving and after arriving (by Confirm,
# then timed); a battle whose first command window waits 600 ms and whose result plays as steps (victory, result,
# level-ups); a vehicle battle in its own front layout; and after a defeat the title's intro again. With --window the
# shot lines save screenshots under build/shots/flows/ (run from bindings/).
