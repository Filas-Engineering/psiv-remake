# The small example set's screens without their own sizes (make_screens_assets.py's SMALL set, 320x224, with --dev):
# a name entry whose pad takes its cell and text sizes from the layout's `pad_*` params and fits the frame; the
# built-in pause laid out by the styles' sizes; then styles with the developer badge at the bottom left, the tools at
# half size with the bitmap font shrunk too, and the music cut on an area change (`music_fade_ms` 0). With --window the
# shot lines save screenshots under build/shots/small/ (run from bindings/).

cmd set text instant
expect screen Title
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 800
dev encounters off
cmd walk guide_front
press Up
wait 3
pressuntil Enter NameEntry 800
wait 2
expect state window.name_entry/box name_entry/box 4 4 312x216
shot build/shots/small/name_pad.png
press Enter
wait 2
pressuntil Enter Field 800

# The built-in pause at 320x224.
press P
wait 2
expect screen Pause
expect state window.pause/box pause/box 8 34 304x156
expect state window.pause/hint pause/hint 0 194 320x30
shot build/shots/small/pause_builtin.png
press Escape
wait 2

# The developer badge and the music fade from the styles.
audio mark
dev warp reed_path 2 2
wait 2
expect audio music field_marsh_reeds fade 800
assets swap ui/styles.json alt/styles_badge.json
wait 2
audio mark
dev warp tidewater 4 2
wait 2
expect audio music town_tide_bells fade 0
shot build/shots/small/dev_badge.png
