# Belt Yard (scripts/make_belt_data.py): an area step time with a fraction and two belts on the same cells, gated by one
# flag, under the real-time field input. Before the flag only the east belt shows (4 drawn, 4 running) and carries the
# leader east at 188 ms a cell; with the flag set the same cells show the west belt, which carries it back at 234 ms.
# No data problem is logged. With --window the shot lines save screenshots under build/shots/belts/.

cmd set text instant
expect screen Title
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 800
dev encounters off
fieldinput realtime
dev warp belt_yard yard_west
wait 10
expect state leader belt_yard 1 2
expect state conveyors 4/4
shot build/shots/belts/east.png
press Right
waitstatehas 60 walkstep carried
expect state walkstep carried 3 2 0/188 100%
waitstate 120 leader belt_yard 6 2
wait 20
expect state leader belt_yard 6 2
dev flag belt.reversed on
wait 2
expect state conveyors 4/4
shot build/shots/belts/west.png
press Left
waitstatehas 60 walkstep carried
expect state walkstep carried 4 2 0/234 100%
waitstate 120 leader belt_yard 1 2
wait 20
expect state leader belt_yard 1 2
