# Narration without a box (make_narration_data.py, make_narration_assets.py), with --dev: the dialogue layout's
# `narration` window gathers the narration lines, four at once, over the still pictures the `picture` tags put on the
# stack, in the narration text style. Each line waits 200 ms (`narration_delay_ms`), then reveals at 40 characters a
# second (`narration_cps` at Normal text speed); Confirm shows the rest, the next Confirm the next line. The fifth line
# starts the window afresh; the guide's line after them comes in the box, the narration gone. With --window the shot
# lines save screenshots under build/shots/narration/ (run from bindings/).

expect screen Title
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 800
dev event narration
waitstate 10 narrationreveal 0/40
# 12 frames of 16 ms: still inside the 200 ms delay.
wait 12
expect state narrationreveal 0/40
expect state pictures chart
# 26 frames: 416 ms, 216 ms past the delay: 8 characters at 40 a second.
wait 14
expect state narrationreveal 8/40
expect state narration The fog
press Enter
expect state narrationreveal 40/40
press Enter
waitstate 10 narrationreveal 0/40
expect state pictures chart tower
press Enter 2
waitstate 10 narrationreveal 0/44
press Enter 2
waitstate 10 narrationreveal 0/27
press Enter
expect state narration The fog came in off the sea before dawn. | By noon the bell tower was a grey shape. | Nobody rang the bell, yet everyone heard it. | The harbor held its breath.
expect state windows dialogue/pictures 340 40 600x260, dialogue/narration 120 200 1040x128
expect state uifield.dialogue.box_visible false
shot build/shots/narration/four_lines.png
# The fifth line does not fit: the window starts afresh.
press Enter
waitstate 10 narrationreveal 0/33
press Enter
expect state narration Then the fog lifted, all at once.
shot build/shots/narration/fresh.png
press Enter
waitstate 10 speaker Harbor Guide
expect state narration none
expect state pictures -
expect state uifield.dialogue.box_visible true
shot build/shots/narration/speaker.png
pressuntil Enter Field 30
