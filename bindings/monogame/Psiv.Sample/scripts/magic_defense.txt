# Magic defense on the data copy whose rules.json sets `magic_defense` (scripts/make_front_data.py --magic-defense): the
# Equip page compares it for the Ward Mantle and the status page lists it; without the switch neither shows it
# (scripts/menus.txt). With --window the shot lines save screenshots under build/shots/ (run from bindings/).

cmd set text instant
expect screen Title
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 800
dev give ward_mantle 1
press M
press Right 2
expect state tab Equip
press Enter
press Down 3
expect state selected body: Padded Coat
press Enter
expect state selected Ward Mantle x1
expect statehas compare Magic defense 0>8
shot build/shots/magic_defense_compare.png
wait 2
press Enter
pressuntil Escape Field 10
press M
expect state tab Status
press Enter
expect state page stats
shot build/shots/magic_defense_status.png
wait 2
pressuntil Escape Field 10
