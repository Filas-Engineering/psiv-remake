# Followers have a cell from the frame the party lands (make_arrival_data.py's copy, game.json `followers_arrive`
# "stacked" and `followers_on_exits` true, a party of two), with --dev: `watch` checks after every frame that the
# follower is in the field's draw list (`actorpos.mystic` not `-`), a stacked follower listed on the leader's cell; a
# Homing Shell used from the generated interior lands on Saltgrass's `return` (stacked), where a load hook's scene warps
# with `followers up` (the cell north of the leader), with no `followers` word (stacked) and with `followers left`
# against a wall (stacked), each line read while the follower stands there; then a step, a dialogue, a developer's warp,
# a save and a load, and a battle's end; then the music through the hold after an area change (make_arrival_data.py's
# map_load_hold_ms), going on when the new area plays the same track. With --window the shot lines save screenshots under build/shots/arrival/ (run
# from bindings/).

cmd set text instant

pressuntil Enter Field 400

expect actor fighter 3 2
expect actor mystic 3 2
