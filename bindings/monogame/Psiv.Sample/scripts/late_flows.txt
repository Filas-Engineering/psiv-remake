# The late layout flows (make_late_flows_assets.py) on the late flows data copy (make_late_flows_data.py), from saves of
# the scripted playthrough and one of them rewritten as save schema 15 (make_old_save.py, manual_5), with --dev: the
# Load screen's blocks with each save's money and its members' hit and technique points (nothing for the old save), the
# hint naming the grid's directions, then the row's; travel from an item's actions over the Items page, the page and
# its actions window drawn under the list and open again after a cancel; a defeat shown in a `defeat` step and the
# result window naming the member the five crushers felled last, without the outcome banner; and the same with Confirm off, the timed
# defeat step going on by itself and the result staying until the styles' return time. With --window the shot lines
# save screenshots under build/shots/late_flows/ (run from bindings/).
