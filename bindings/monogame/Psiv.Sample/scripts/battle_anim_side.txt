# The example animation set (scripts/make_anim_assets.py) in the side layout, on the front data copy with --dev: the
# stepped fade and split reveal into battle, the blade's hit on frame 42, the heal's window, the hound pulling a member
# under it, a status's tick, the staff's teleport and the boss's next form, Frost Pane's releases six frames apart, the
# instant defeat's mark and bands, the victory and the tiles out. Then the combination and the two-weapon variants
# played live: `equip` puts a second blade in the fighter's left hand and then a two-handed blade in its hands, and
# `combo` has the medic and the mystic cast Twin Arc, in the side layout and again in the front one. After each round
# the playback matches the session (battlesync). With --window the shot lines save screenshots under build/shots/.
