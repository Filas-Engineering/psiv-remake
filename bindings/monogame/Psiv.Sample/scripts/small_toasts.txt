# The small example layout set's notices on the menu, the shop and the Load screen (make_layout_assets.py --small,
# 320x224): their text takes the styles' `small` text size, as the field's notices do: 16 in the set's styles, then 10
# with a styles file whose small size is 10. With --window the shot lines save screenshots under build/shots/small/
# (run from bindings/).

cmd set text instant
expect screen Title
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 800
dev encounters off
cmd walk guide_front
press Up
wait 3
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 800
wait 10
shot build/shots/small/toasts_field.png

waitstatehas 120 toastboxes text 16:
shot build/shots/small/toasts_menu.png
assets swap ui/styles.json alt/styles_small10.json
waitstatehas 30 toastboxes text 10:

press Enter
