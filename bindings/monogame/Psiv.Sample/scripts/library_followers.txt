# Followers drawn from the session's trail (Followers()) on data whose game.json sets `followers_arrive` "behind"
# (make_library_data.py), from saves of the scripted playthrough, with --dev and the built-in field layout (no
# `followers_arrive` param): after a warp each follower stands behind the one before as the session places them, the
# session leaving out exit cells and stopping at a wall; then they trail the leader cell by cell. With --window the shot
# lines save screenshots under build/shots/library/ (run from bindings/).

cmd set text instant
expect screen Title
wait 2
cmd load manual_1
wait 4
expect screen Field
dev encounters off
fieldinput realtime
dev warp reed_path 10 4
wait 5
expect state leader reed_path 10 4
expect actor fighter 10 4 north
expect actor mystic 10 5
expect actor medic 10 6
shot build/shots/library/followers_warp.png
# Walking on: the line follows the session's trail, the drawn leader a step ahead of it.
press Right
wait 20
expect actor fighter 11 4
expect actor mystic 10 4
expect actor medic 10 5
press Down
wait 20
expect actor fighter 11 5
expect actor mystic 11 4
expect actor medic 10 4
shot build/shots/library/followers_trail.png
# A warp facing south: the line behind goes north while those cells can be walked.
dev warp reed_path west_gate
wait 5
expect state leader reed_path 1 4
expect actor mystic 1 3
shot build/shots/library/followers_edge.png
# The developer console's `rest` (DevRest): a free rest, marking the game.
dev rest
expect state consolelast rested: hit and technique points refilled
