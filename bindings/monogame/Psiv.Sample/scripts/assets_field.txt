# Run with --dev on a copy of build/test_assets (make_test_assets.py), since it changes a file there: the reed path's
# whole-area picture by the maps/ file convention with its above picture over the party, Ashfall Market's picture at 32
# pixels a cell with the mended bridge from its change's picture and the sealed shrine from tiles, objects with pictures
# of their own at Ember Steps, the reed path's chest closed and opened at another size and the Fell Vault's door opened
# by its flag, Saltgrass Market's animated tile with its wall shadows and water foam off, the fighter's sheet at three
# times the size, battle figures with a scale (snapped sizes in both layouts), the bog wisps at their natural size on
# their foot row without a shadow, the bog wisps' own attack look and their fallen pose, battles aboard the reed
# skimmer on water with the water backdrop and the skimmer's overlay in both layouts, and the reed path's picture
# swapped and reloaded. With --window the shot lines save screenshots under build/shots/ (run from bindings/); headless
# runs write nothing for them.

cmd set text instant
expect screen Title
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 800
cmd walk guide_front
press Up
wait 3
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 400
dev encounters off
expect asset character:fighter file
waitstate 30 fieldpicture -

# The reed path: its picture and the reeds above it, drawn over the party standing under them; the long chest.
dev warp reed_path lookout_front
wait 2
expect asset map:reed_path file
expect asset map:reed_path:above file
expect asset map:ashfall_market file
waitstate 30 fieldpicture reed_path above patched 0 tiled 0
expect actor fighter 1 2
waitstate 30 objects reed_path:reed_cache closed 2x1 frame 0
shot build/shots/assets_map_reed.png
dev flag chest.reed_cache on
wait 2
waitstate 30 objects reed_path:reed_cache open 2x2 frame 0
cmd walk cache_front
wait 160
shot build/shots/assets_map_reed_chest.png

# Ashfall Market at 32 pixels a cell: the canal as drawn, then the bridge from its change's picture, then the sealed
# shrine, whose change has no picture, from tiles.
dev warp ashfall_market bridge_west
wait 2
waitstate 30 fieldpicture ashfall_market patched 0 tiled 0
shot build/shots/assets_map_ashfall.png
dev flag fells.bridge_mended on
wait 2
waitstate 30 fieldpicture ashfall_market patched 1 tiled 0
dev flag fells.shrine_sealed on
wait 2
waitstate 30 fieldpicture ashfall_market patched 1 tiled 1
shot build/shots/assets_map_ashfall_changed.png
