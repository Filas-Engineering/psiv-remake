# Headless smoke run of the sample's screens: title, the arrival fade played as a timed animation the
# event waits on, dialogue and a choice, a name entry, walking with held keys, the quest log, the field menu

# everything else is key presses through the same controller as the window. A direction tap is followed by
# a short wait, since the field plays it at its next tick.
