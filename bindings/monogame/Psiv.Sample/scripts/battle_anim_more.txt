# The battle animation additions with the set `make_anim_assets.py --more` writes, on the front data copy with --dev.
# Its battle layout moves at half the distances (motion_percent 50). The fighter's blade script: the fighter slides
# 40 logical pixels ("40px", never scaled) while the other members drop 40 scaled pixels (20 at 50%), hides from the
# banner and shows again from `animate` (a later pose lifts `hidden`), warms the backdrop with added light and opacity,
# starts five flashes inside a second of which the limit keeps three, shakes 6 logical pixels, and turns its release
# cues off; the felled enemy fades on into the next turn (`outlast`). Then the layout's slots out of marching order
# with and without the step-aside, the effectiveness, critical and miss marks turned off, reduced flash dropping a
# sprite and replacing a flash, and a worn piece's `item:` key. With --window the shot lines save screenshots under
# build/shots/more/.

press Enter

dev battle training

expect state overlaps none
expect state partyplaces Test Fighter 136 588, Test Mystic 656 588, Test Medic 808 588
shot build/shots/more/command.png
