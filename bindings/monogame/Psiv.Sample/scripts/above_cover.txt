# The area's above picture over everything standing on the map (scripts/make_scene_assets.py's ABOVE copy, on the
# background data copy): Wren Yard's above picture is opaque magenta on every other cell. The guide's party joins (two
# followers), the skimmer is parked in the yard, the goat and the lamplighter wander, the trader keeps the stall and the
# bell is a sign; in Wren Hollow the tallykeeper stands behind its counter (its talk point's actor_at). `abovecover` compares, for each thing drawn on the map, every pixel where the above picture is opaque
# with the frame's pixel there: it must be the above picture's, standing still and mid-step. `pixel.X.Y` and
# `cellpixel.X.Y` read single pixels. With --window the shot lines save screenshots under build/shots/above/.

cmd set text instant
expect screen Title
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 800
dev encounters off
cmd walk guide_front
press Up
wait 3
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 800
dev warp wren_yard yard_middle
dev vehicle reed_skimmer wren_yard pen_side
wait 30
expect state fieldpicture wren_yard above patched 0 tiled 0
expect state vehicles reed_skimmer 5 5
fieldinput realtime
hold Left 20
expect statehas walkstep west
expect statenot abovecover uncovered
hold Up 12
wait 40
expect statenot abovecover uncovered
expect statehas abovecover sprites
shot build/shots/above/yard.png
# Even cells are covered, odd ones show the ground.
expect statehas cellpixel.6.4 ff00ff
expect statehas cellpixel.6.3 466e3c
hold Right 9
expect statehas walkstep east
expect statenot abovecover uncovered
shot build/shots/above/mid_step.png
wait 120
expect statenot abovecover uncovered
dev warp wren_hollow hollow_door
wait 30
expect actor tallykeeper 4 1
expect statenot abovecover uncovered
shot build/shots/above/hollow.png
