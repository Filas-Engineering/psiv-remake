# Run with --dev on build/more_assets: a copy of build/test_assets with alt/assets_more.json as its manifest
# (make_test_assets.py), since the run swaps a file there. The title's own background, logo, drifting clouds, flapping
# birds, traveler and creature, then the same art at its natural size placed by a title layout of its own; the guide's sheet with two facings, facing east until a script turns it, then west drawn
# as east flipped and north drawn from its fallback, south; battle backdrops by Tidewater Landing's regions, by the reed
# path's picture of colors, by the ash storm's flag on the reed path only, by the kind where neither gives one, and the
# reed path's picture swapped and reloaded. With --window the shot lines save screenshots under build/shots/assets_more/
# (run from bindings/); headless runs write nothing for them.

waitstate 30 titleart background logo layers 2 traveler creature
