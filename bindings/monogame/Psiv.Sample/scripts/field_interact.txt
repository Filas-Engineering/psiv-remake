# The real-time field input's interaction on the shipped data: Confirm, and the Interact key, act on the faced cell, or
# else on a neighboring cell that answers (the sides before the back), turning the leader to it first; the prompt (the
# key and a word in the field layout's `prompt` window, and the pointer bobbing over the target) shows while the leader
# stands, not while it walks, and goes when the option is turned off; the options page has the two real-time options
# and the Controls page the three real-time actions, rebound like the others. With --window the shot lines save
# screenshots under build/shots/field/ (run from bindings/).

cmd set text instant
expect screen Title
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 400
fieldinput realtime
expect state interact_prompt on

# Beside the stall, facing away from it: the prompt names it, and Confirm turns the leader and opens the shop.
cmd walk 7 2
cmd face west
wait 2
expect state interacttarget shop at 8 2 facing east
expect state interactprompt shop at 8 2
expect state window.prompt field/prompt 580 644 120x28
shot build/shots/field/interact_prompt_shop.png
press Enter
wait 2
expect screen Shop
expect actor fighter 7 2 east
pressuntil Escape Field 20

# Below the stall, facing east: the side is taken before the back, and Interact (E) works as Confirm does.
cmd walk 8 3
cmd face east
wait 2
expect state interacttarget shop at 8 2 facing north
press E
wait 2
expect screen Shop
pressuntil Escape Field 20

# Walking into the crate stops at it; Confirm opens it without lining up.
cmd walk 11 5
wait 2
hold Down 24
wait 10
expect actor fighter 11 6 south
expect state interactprompt open at 11 7
press Enter
wait 4
expect toast Tonic
wait 4
expect state interactprompt none
shot build/shots/field/interact_crate_open.png

# Nothing to act on: no prompt, and Confirm says so.
cmd walk 6 4
wait 2
expect state interacttarget none
expect state window.prompt -
press Enter
wait 2
expect toast There is nothing there

# The prompt waits while the leader walks.
cmd walk 7 2
wait 2
expect state interactprompt shop at 8 2
hold Down 3
expect state interactprompt none
wait 20

# The options: the two real-time rows, before Controls; the prompt turned off and on again.
press P
expect screen Pause
press Down
press Enter
expect screen Options
press Up 2
expect state selected Interaction prompt: On
shot build/shots/field/interact_options.png

press Up
