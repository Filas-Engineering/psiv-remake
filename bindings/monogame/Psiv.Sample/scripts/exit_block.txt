# The exits' rule on a 2x2 block of exits (make_library_data.py: exit_yard_a and exit_yard_b, each block leading onto the
# other's, where the party arrives standing on an exit), with --dev: the arrival on the block leaves nobody in a loop;
# walking across the block's other cells takes no exit; stepping off onto floor and back on takes it. The same with keys
# and with a click (the session's WalkTo, which crosses exit cells that would not fire and takes one only as its goal),
# in the real-time and the classic field input. With --window the shot lines save screenshots under build/shots/library/
# (run from bindings/).

cmd set text instant
expect screen Title
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 800
dev encounters off

# Real time, keys (a tap walks one cell): into the block from floor takes the exit, onto the other yard's block.
fieldinput realtime
dev warp exit_yard_a 5 3
wait 5
press Right
wait 25
expect state leader exit_yard_b 6 3
shot build/shots/library/exit_arrived.png
# Across the block: east, then south, staying in the yard.
press Right
wait 20
expect state leader exit_yard_b 7 3
press Down
wait 20
expect state leader exit_yard_b 7 4
shot build/shots/library/exit_across.png
# Off onto floor and back on: the exit takes the party.
press Right
wait 20
expect state leader exit_yard_b 8 4
press Left
wait 25
expect state leader exit_yard_a 6 3

# Real time, a click: a goal across the block walks round it without leaving, then one on the block from floor leaves.
dev warp exit_yard_a 2 3
wait 5
clickcell 8 4
wait 200
expect state leader exit_yard_a 8 4
clickcell 7 4
wait 40
expect state leader exit_yard_b 6 3
shot build/shots/library/exit_click.png

# Classic input (a held key steps a cell per step time): the same crossing and the same exit.
fieldinput classic
dev warp exit_yard_a 5 3
wait 5
hold Right 12
wait 20
expect state leader exit_yard_b 6 3
hold Right 12
wait 20
expect state leader exit_yard_b 7 3
hold Down 20
wait 20
expect state leader exit_yard_b 7 4
hold Right 20
wait 20
expect state leader exit_yard_b 8 4
hold Left 20
wait 25
expect state leader exit_yard_a 6 3
dev warp exit_yard_b 2 3
wait 5
clickcell 8 3
wait 200
expect state leader exit_yard_b 8 3
clickcell 7 3
wait 40
expect state leader exit_yard_a 6 3
shot build/shots/library/exit_classic.png
