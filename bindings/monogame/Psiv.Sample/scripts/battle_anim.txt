# The battle animation scripts (docs/battle-animation.md) with the example set scripts/make_anim_assets.py writes, on the
# front data copy with --dev. Seed 1. Each moment's script frames are asserted from the animation log: the stepped
# fade and split reveal into battle, the blade attack's hit on frame 42, the heal, the hound pulling a member under it,
# a status's tick, the staff's teleport and the boss's next form with its recolor and backdrop swap, the frost's three

# playback matches the session (battlesync). With --window the shot lines save screenshots under build/shots/.
