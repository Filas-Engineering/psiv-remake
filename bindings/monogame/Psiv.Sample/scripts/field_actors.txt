# Script actors through the sample's built-in animations, on the data copy scripts/make_field_data.py writes: an actor
# named after the marsh encounter stands on its talk point in Tidewater Landing drawn as that encounter's field enemy;
# talking to it plays `field_creature`, which hops it, trembles it, moves it at twice the walking pace, fades it out,
# fades it in and blinks it out, then fades it in and dissolves it, each vanishing followed by the hide that removes it.
# With --window the shot lines save screenshots under build/shots/field/ (run from bindings/).

cmd set text instant
expect screen Title
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 400
fieldinput realtime
cmd walk 10 5
wait 2
expect actor marsh 10 6
expect state interacttarget talk at 10 6 facing south
shot build/shots/field/actor_creature.png

# Confirm turns the leader to the creature below it and talks to it.
press Enter
expect actor fighter 10 5 south
expect pose marsh jump
wait 12
shot build/shots/field/actor_hop.png
wait 24
expect pose marsh tremble
shot build/shots/field/actor_tremble.png
wait 40
expect actor marsh walking
wait 60
expect pose marsh fadeout
shot build/shots/field/actor_fade_out.png
wait 40
expect actor marsh hidden

# Hidden, it walks back to its talk point unseen; shown again it fades in, then blinks out.
wait 75
expect pose marsh fadein
expect actor marsh 10 6
shot build/shots/field/actor_fade_in.png
wait 30
expect pose marsh blinkout
shot build/shots/field/actor_blink_out.png
wait 45
expect pose marsh fadein
wait 30
expect pose marsh dissolve
shot build/shots/field/actor_dissolve.png
wait 50
expect screen Field
expect actor marsh hidden
