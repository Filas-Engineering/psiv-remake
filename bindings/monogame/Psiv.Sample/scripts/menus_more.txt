# The menu follow-ups (make_menus_more_assets.py), with --dev: a tab's children (the list under the tab, a child page
# opened and Back to the list), larger tabs, abilities not usable in the field hidden and then dimmed again after a
# reload, the party talk shown on its page, and the System action opening the system page alone over the field, listed
# on the Controls page and rebound. With --window the shot lines save screenshots under build/shots/menus_more/ (run
# from bindings/).
