# The mouse and the window: a click walks the leader to a cell through the session's path walk at walking pace, a key
# stops it, a click on a wall finds no way, and a click on the town's east exit walks out onto the reed path, where
# party talk comes up by itself as toasts that do not stop play. Then how the 1280x720 frame fits windows of other
# sizes: whole-number scales with bars when there is room, shrunk to fit when there is not. With --window, the window
# lines resize the window and the shot lines save screenshots under build/shots/ (run from bindings/).

cmd set text instant
expect screen Title
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 400
cmd walk guide_front
press Up
wait 3
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 800
expect state party Test Fighter, Test Mystic, Test Medic

# Two cells right and two down: the walk takes a step each 250 ms, so it is still going after 20 frames.
clickcell 7 5
expect state walk 7 5
wait 20
expect actor fighter walking
shot build/shots/window_click_walk.png
wait 60
expect state walk -
expect actor fighter 7 5

# A held direction takes the leader back at once.
clickcell 10 6
wait 2
expect state walk 10 6
press Left
expect state walk -

# A wall has no way to it.
clickcell 0 0
wait 2
expect toast There is no way there.
expect state walk -

# Out of the east gate: the walk ends on the exit, the reed path's arrival brings its party talk as toasts, and the
# field stays under the player's control.
clickcell 13 4
wait 120
expect state area Reed Path
expect state walk -
expect toast Test Mystic: Every step out here squelches.
expect toast Test Medic: Count the bites instead.
expect screen Field
shot build/shots/window_ambient_talk.png

window 1600 1000

window 960 540

window 1280 720
