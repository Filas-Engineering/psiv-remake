# Followers placed by the exit taken and a wanderer's talk point that answers where she stands, on the library data copy
# made with --moving (make_library_data.py), from saves of the scripted playthrough, with --dev and the built-in field
# layout: Follow Yard's exit with `followers` "behind" (to Follow Room) puts the line behind the leader against its arrival facing and the
# one with "up" puts it north of the leader; the ashwife wanders with `talk_at` "actor" and `turns`, the prompt points at
# the cell she stands on, a click on her walks up to her and faces her without talking (the field layout's `click_actor`
# is "walk" by default), and Confirm talks to her there while she turns to the leader and stands still.

cmd set text instant
expect screen Title
wait 2
cmd load manual_1
wait 4
expect screen Field
dev encounters off
fieldinput realtime
# The `behind` exit: east onto it from beside it, arriving at the middle facing east, the line to the west.
dev warp follow_yard behind_side
wait 5
press Right
wait 20
expect state leader follow_room 6 4
expect actor mystic 5 4
expect actor medic 4 4
# The `up` exit: the line goes north of the leader, as far as the wall lets it.
dev warp follow_yard up_side
wait 5
press Right
wait 20
expect state leader follow_room 6 4
expect actor mystic 6 3
expect actor medic 6 2
# The ashwife, wandering near her home (3, 2).
dev warp follow_yard 8 6
wait 30
expect state walkstep -
expect actor ashwife 2 2 west
# Beside her: the prompt points at her cell.
dev warp follow_yard 2 3
wait 2
expect statehas interactprompt at 2 2
expect state interacttarget talk at 2 2 facing north
# She walks on to the west: diagonal to the leader now, nothing to prompt; beside her again, the prompt is on her new cell.
wait 62
expect actor ashwife 1 2
expect state interactprompt none
dev warp follow_yard 1 3
wait 2
expect statehas interactprompt at 1 2
# A click on her from across the yard walks up to her and faces her; the default `click_actor` "walk" talks to nobody.
dev warp follow_yard 6 6
wait 2
expect actor ashwife 1 2
clickcell 1 2
wait 120
expect screen Field
expect state leader follow_yard 3 1
wait 12
expect actor fighter 3 1 west
expect state interacttarget talk at 2 1 facing west
# Confirm talks to her where she stands: she turns to the leader and stands still while the line is up.
press Enter
wait 5
expect screen Dialogue
expect actor ashwife 2 1 east
wait 60
expect actor ashwife 2 1 east
pressuntil Enter Field 200
expect screen Field
