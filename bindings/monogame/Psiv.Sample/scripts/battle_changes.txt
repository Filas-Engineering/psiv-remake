# The battle screen under the newer rules, on the data copy scripts/make_front_data.py writes, with --dev for the
# commands that reach each moment. Seed 1. In the front layout: the Hive Lantern calling a Gel Mite into the place beside
# it (the stage spreads over the new place, the label, bar and target pointer follow), two Twin Oozes merging into one,
# the Tide Colossus taking its next form, the arena's replacements stepping into a fallen mite's place, an instant defeat
# landing on a lone Twin Ooze with its mark and being shrugged off by the shielded boss, whose shield the Sunstone takes
# away; the Cogwright's Calm Tide restoring technique points, shown as TP; the mystic's worn Storm Rod listed among the
# items and cast without being used up; and in the side layout the summon and the next form again. After each round the
# playback matches the session (battlesync), and the developer win resyncs the stage. With --window the shot lines save
# screenshots under build/shots/ (run from bindings/); headless runs write nothing for them.

expect screen Title

cmd walk guide_front
