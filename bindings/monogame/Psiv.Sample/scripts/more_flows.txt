# The second layout flows (make_more_flows_assets.py) on the more flows data copy (make_more_flows_data.py), from saves of
# the scripted playthrough, with --dev: the title's intro scrolling a manifest picture through an image element's `src`
# with `repeat` (one band every 225 ms, wrapping), cropping a title art layer's frame and, on the menu, the right half of
# a PNG; the Load screen as slot blocks in a grid two wide (Left, Right, Up, Down; an empty slot's own template refusing
# Confirm) and in a row (wrapping), with each party's sprites and levels; the inn's decline line on leaving and its
# farewell after a stay; the shop's question alone before the YES/NO (the first press never buys, `question_ms` opening
# it by itself) and the plain confirm step not buying on the press that opened it; travel over the field menu, a town use
# listing its one town and the way out of a dungeon going at once by kind, then the Homing Shell at once by its id; the
# level-ups two lines a page with the UI skin's page arrow cropped; a vehicle battle's grouped Weapons row opening its
# skills in a window of their own; and after a defeat the intro from its logo step. With --window the shot lines save
# screenshots under build/shots/more_flows/ (run from bindings/).
