# The board by the inn: a scene that walks two party members in before they are in the lineup, then adds them, so
# the party is full. Both are drawn with their own sprites throughout, never as a label; when the scene ends they
# walk back to the last two places in the line, and the line then trails all four followers. With --window, the
# shot lines save screenshots under build/shots/ (run from bindings/); headless runs write nothing for them.

expect screen Title
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 400
cmd walk guide_front
press Up
wait 3
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 400
# Along the row to the board, so the mystic and the medic trail the leader on it.
cmd walk 7 3
hold Left 100
expect actor fighter 2 3 west
expect actor mystic 3 3
expect actor medic 4 3
press Enter
wait 2
expect screen Dialogue
shot build/shots/full_1_board.png
press Enter
# The engineer, not in the lineup, has no place until the scene's first move puts it at the east gate; the second
# walks it along the row to the cell below the leader.
wait 60
expect actor engineer walking
expect actor scout hidden
expect label none
shot build/shots/full_2_engineer_walking.png
wait 120
expect actor engineer 2 4 north
expect pose engineer wave
expect label none
shot build/shots/full_3_engineer_waves.png
wait 60
expect screen Dialogue
press Enter
# The scout comes in the same way, to the cell beside the engineer, turns toward the leader and nods.
wait 60
expect actor scout walking
expect label none
wait 110
expect actor scout 3 4 north
expect pose scout nod marked
shot build/shots/full_4_scout_nods.png
wait 50
expect screen Dialogue
press Enter
# Both join and the scene ends. The leader is the player's at once; the scout walks back to the third place in the
# line and the engineer to the fourth, a cell per step along x and then y.
wait 2
expect screen Field
expect toast Test Scout joins
expect actor fighter 2 3 west
expect actor scout walking
expect actor engineer walking
shot build/shots/full_5_falling_in.png
wait 90
expect actor mystic 3 3
expect actor medic 4 3
expect actor scout 5 3 west
expect actor engineer 6 3 west
shot build/shots/full_6_in_line.png
# Down the column with a full party: every member trails the leader, four followers deep.
hold Down 40
wait 20
expect actor fighter 2 5 south
expect actor mystic 2 4
expect actor medic 2 3
expect actor scout 3 3
expect actor engineer 4 3
shot build/shots/full_7_trail.png
