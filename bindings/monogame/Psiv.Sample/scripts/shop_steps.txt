# The shop in steps (make_shop_assets.py, with --dev): Buy or Sell, the list, a quantity with its total and the keeper's
# confirm line, then the result with the keeper's thanks or cannot-afford line; the trades' outcomes go to the layout's
# `result_text`, not the notices; Back goes a step back and leaves from the choice. Then the tab layout with a
# `result_text` line (alt/shop_tabs.json). With --window the shot lines save screenshots under build/shots/shop/ (run
# from bindings/).

cmd set text instant
expect screen Title
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 800
dev encounters off
wait 300
cmd walk stall_front
press Up
wait 3
press Enter
expect screen Shop
wait 2
expect state uifield.shop.step choose
expect state voice ui.shop.welcome
shot build/shots/shop/choose.png
press Enter
wait 2
expect state uifield.shop.step list
expect state selected Tonic
press Enter
wait 2
expect state uifield.shop.step quantity
expect state voice ui.shop.confirm
press Right 2
wait 2
expect state uifield.shop.quantity 3

shot build/shots/shop/quantity.png
press Enter
wait 2
