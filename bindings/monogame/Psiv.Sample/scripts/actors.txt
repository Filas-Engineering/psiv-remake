# A scripted actor walk: the arrival walks the dockhand from its talk point by the dock over to the leader, turns
# it to speak, and walks it back. The session reports each move's end cell at once; the sample walks the sprite
# there a cell per step and holds the move until it arrives, so the event waits on the walk. With --window, the
# shot lines save screenshots under build/shots/ (run from bindings/); headless runs write nothing for them.
