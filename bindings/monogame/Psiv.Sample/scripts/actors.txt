# A scripted actor walk: the arrival walks the dockhand from its talk point by the dock over to the leader, turns
# it to speak, and walks it back. The session reports each move's end cell at once; the sample walks the sprite
# there a cell per step and holds the move until it arrives, so the event waits on the walk. With --window, the
# shot lines save screenshots under build/shots/ (run from bindings/); headless runs write nothing for them.

expect screen Title
press Enter
# The fade in, then the ferry narration. The dockhand and the guide stand at their talk points.
wait 50
expect screen Dialogue
expect actor dockhand 10 2
expect actor guide 5 2
shot build/shots/actors_1_home.png
press Enter
# A cell down from the talk point (250 ms), then along the row toward the leader (six cells, 1500 ms).
wait 40
expect actor dockhand walking
shot build/shots/actors_2_walking.png
wait 80
expect actor dockhand 5 4
expect screen Dialogue
shot build/shots/actors_3_greeting.png
press Enter
# Back along the row, up to the dock and onto the talk point.
wait 50
expect actor dockhand walking
shot build/shots/actors_4_returning.png
wait 100
expect screen Field
expect actor dockhand 10 2
shot build/shots/actors_5_back.png
# Talking to the guide turns it toward the leader; the guide already faces south, so it stays put.
cmd walk guide_front
press Up
wait 3
press Enter
expect screen Dialogue
expect actor guide 5 2
pressuntil Enter Field 400
expect actor guide 5 2
expect actor dockhand 10 2
