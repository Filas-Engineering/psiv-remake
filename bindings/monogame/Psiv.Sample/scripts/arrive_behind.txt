# Followers on arrival with the field layout's `followers_arrive` "behind" (scripts/make_scene_assets.py's SCENE copy,
# its alt/field_behind.json swapped in, so hot reload too): after an exit, a warp and a load each follower stands a cell
# behind the one before, against the leader's facing, at once; a cell that cannot be walked leaves the rest stacked on
# the leader. Without the param (field_trail.txt) they stack as before. Screenshots under build/shots/arrive/ with --window.

cmd set text instant
expect screen Title
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 800
dev encounters off
cmd walk guide_front
press Up
wait 3
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 800
assets swap ui/field.json alt/field_behind.json
# An exit: Tidewater's east gate to the reed path's west gate, facing east; the cell behind the exit's is the map's edge.
cmd walk east_gate
hold Right 20
wait 30
expect state leader reed_path 1 4
expect actor fighter 1 4 east
expect actor mystic 0 4 east
expect actor medic hidden
shot build/shots/arrive/exit.png
# A warp within the area.
dev warp reed_path 10 4
wait 5
expect actor fighter 10 4 east
expect actor mystic 9 4 east
expect actor medic 8 4 east
shot build/shots/arrive/warp.png
# A load from the title, saved two cells further on.
cmd walk 12 4
cmd menu
cmd save manual_1
cmd close
dev warp reed_path 5 6
wait 5
cmd title
wait 3
cmd load manual_1
wait 5
expect state leader reed_path 12 4
expect actor mystic 11 4 east
expect actor medic 10 4 east
shot build/shots/arrive/load.png
