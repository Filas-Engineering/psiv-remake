# The title's music after its intro and an arrival event through the map load hold (make_arrival_hold_data.py's copy,
# make_arrival_hold_assets.py's set, with --dev): Enter skips the intro and `intro_end_music` puts its track in place of
# the title's; a Homing Shell lands on Saltgrass's `return`, whose load hook speaks, warps to Tidewater and speaks again;
# under the field's `map_hold_events` "wait" the event waits through the hold (300 ms) and its fade-in (250 ms): no line
# while the screen is dark, the landing area's track starting as the fade-in begins, so it plays before the event warps
# the party onward, and the same again after the warp. With --window the shot lines save screenshots under
# build/shots/arrival_hold/ (run from bindings/).

cmd set text instant
expect screen Title
wait 1
expect state uifield.title.intro true
expect state music title_harbor_dawn
audio mark
wait 10
press Enter
wait 2
expect state uifield.title.intro false
expect state music victory_bright_sail
expect audio music victory_bright_sail
pressuntil Enter Field 400
wait 2
expect state music field_marsh_reeds
dev encounters off

# The shell lands on the field; the arrival event waits under the dark screen, the field's track starts with the fade-in.
press M
press Right
wait 3
expect state tab Items
press Enter
wait 4
expect screen Travel
audio mark
press Enter
wait 2
expect state leader arrival_field 3 4
expect state line -
expect state music -
shot build/shots/arrival_hold/held.png

waitstate 30 maphold 0

expect state music town_canal_market

shot build/shots/arrival_hold/line.png
