# What the sample plays, read from the recording audio layer's call log: the title's and each area's music, the
# battle's track and the area's back after it, the cues of menu actions, refusals, saves, footsteps and battle events,
# volume changes from Options applied at once, and a scene's music and wind after a skip and after a load. Each
# `audio mark` starts a new window of calls for the `expect audio` and `expect noaudio` lines after it.

cmd set text instant
wait 1
expect screen Title
expect state music title
expect audio music title fade 800
expect audio volumes 80 80 80
audio mark
press Down
expect audio cue cursor
press Up
audio mark
press Enter
expect audio cue confirm
wait 2
expect state music town_tidewater
expect audio music town_tidewater fade 800
pressuntil Enter Field 400
expect audio cue line

# Talking to nothing is refused, with the refusal's cue and no confirm.
audio mark
press Enter
expect toast There is nothing there.
expect audio cue refuse
expect noaudio cue confirm
audio mark
press M
expect audio cue menu_open
press Right
expect audio cue cursor
press Escape
expect screen Field
expect audio cue menu_close
audio mark
hold Right 30
expect audio cue step

# Music and ambient volume down a step each from Options over the pause overlay: each applies at once.
press P
expect screen Pause
press Down
press Enter
expect screen Options
press Down 8
expect state selected Music volume: 80
audio mark
press Left
expect state volumes 70 80 80
expect audio volumes 70 80 80
press Down
press Left
expect audio volumes 70 70 80
press Escape
press Escape
expect screen Field
press M
press Right 3
expect state tab Save
audio mark
press Enter
expect toast Saved to manual_0.
expect audio cue save
press Escape
expect screen Field

# The guide's apprentices join, so the party wins the reed path's battle below.

expect audio cue victory
