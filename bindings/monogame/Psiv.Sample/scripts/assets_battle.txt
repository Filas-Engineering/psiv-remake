# battle's own window frame, extra poses and the party slots' status looks. The tide chart's scene: the fighter bows by
# its sheet's pose of that name alone, and the medic's sheet has no shrug, so it stands still and the transcript says so
# once. The scene's battle in the front layout: the fighter's Focus Shot plays the flourish pose of its back figure, a
# gel mite's attack its wobble, and the battle's windows take the rust brown battle frame while the dialogue keeps the menu
# frame. With the battle layouts swapped for ones with a statuses table: the side layout, where the fighter's side
# figure has no flourish (a transcript line, and its attack plays) and the hound's venom tints its name green; then the
# marsh in the front layout, where sleep tints the medic's name blue and shows no icon while the poisoned fighter's
# stays green. Last, the battle frame replaced by the menu's on the fly. With --window, the shot lines save screenshots
# under build/shots/assets_battle/ (run from bindings/); headless runs write nothing.
