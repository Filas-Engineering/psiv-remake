# The Cogway and the battle options of this round, with --dev on the shipped data. Seed 1. The belts drawn still while
# the hall is off and running once its lever is thrown, the gate to the moor a wall until then, the welcome that plays
# once, the nameless sweeper's lines without a name plate and the way it frees once paid, the music box changing the
# hall's tune, and in battle a formation with free places on each side of the jelly. With --window the shot lines save
# screenshots under build/shots/ (run from bindings/); headless runs write nothing for them.

cmd set text instant
expect screen Title
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 800
dev encounters off
dev warp cogway 1 4
wait 10
expect state area Cogway
expect state conveyors 4/4
expect state music dungeon_kiln_halls
shot build/shots/cogway_off.png

# The lever: the gate opens and the welcome plays once.
press Left
wait 3
press Enter
wait 4
expect state speaker none
pressuntil Enter Field 400
expect state leader cogway 1 4
shot build/shots/cogway_on.png

# The sweeper stands in the way, speaks with no name plate, and steps aside once paid.
dev money 40
cmd walk 8 3
press Up
wait 3
press Enter
wait 4
expect state speaker none
shot build/shots/cogway_sweeper.png
press Enter
wait 3
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 400
cmd walk 8 2
wait 10
expect state leader cogway 8 2

# The music box sets the hall's tune until the party leaves.
cmd walk 10 1
press Up
wait 3
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 400
expect state music dungeon_firing_floor

# A belt carries the party along its row.
dev warp cogway 2 3
wait 60
expect state leader cogway 6 3
shot build/shots/cogway_belt.png

# Free places in a formation: the jelly stands in the middle of three, in both layouts.
dev battle jelly
expect mode Battle
waitstate 120 battlephase Command
expect state formation 3: 1
shot build/shots/cogway_formation_front.png
battlelayout side
wait 4
expect state formation 3: 1
shot build/shots/cogway_formation_side.png
dev win
waitstate 400 battlephase Ended
pressuntil Enter Field 600
