# The Ashen Fells and the party features of the latest round, on the data copy scripts/make_front_data.py writes, with
# --dev for the commands that reach each moment. Seed 1. The dock at Cinder Quay appearing once its flag is set, the
# lantern barge walked aboard with its helm and gangway, the helm's destination screen; Ashfall Market's weapon shop
# under its own label; the Equip page with a knife in the left hand beside the blade, the two-handed cleaver's
# comparison sending both back, and a shield in each hand; the bridge mended and the market emptied by the ash storm;
# the moor's soft ground and barrier wall, a vehicle called from the Items page; in the front battle layout the kites
# hovering over their shadows and their joint attack's name plate clear of every party figure, and the attack again in
# the side layout; and the Cogwright
