# The real-time field input's walking, with --dev on the shipped data: a tap walks exactly one cell and the glide
# starts on the frame of the press; a held direction glides cell after cell at a steady 48 pixels per step time with
# no stop between cells (the trace lines print the leader's drawn position each frame); a direction tapped during a
# step goes next and the held one carries on after it; of two held, the latest pressed wins and letting it go falls
# back to the other; Run held, and Always run toggled by RunToggle, walk at the layouts' run speed; the D-pad and the
# stick, whose hysteresis keeps a direction held near a diagonal; a wall only turns the leader; aboard the skimmer the
# steps take the vehicle's own time, and a belt's carry glides over the belt's own time. Then `fieldinput classic`
# gives back the old field, where a tap only turns the leader. With --window the shot lines save screenshots under
# build/shots/field/ (run from bindings/).

cmd set text instant
expect screen Title
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 400
dev encounters off
fieldinput realtime
expect state fieldinput realtime
cmd walk 3 4
wait 2
expect state leaderpos 144 192

# A tap: the step starts on the press and the leader stands on the next cell one step time later.
trace leaderpos,walkstep
press Right
expect state walkstep east 4 4 32/250 100%
expect state leaderpos 150.1 192
wait 15
trace off
expect actor fighter 4 4 east
expect state walkstep -

# Held: three cells at 250 ms each, the glide never stopping between them.
trace leaderpos,walkstep,held
hold Right 46
trace off
wait 16
expect actor fighter 7 4 east
shot build/shots/field/walk_held.png

# Down tapped during a step to the right goes next; Right, still held, carries on after it.
hold Right 4
hold Right+Down 1
expect state walkbuffer south
expect state held east south
hold Right 40
wait 20
expect actor fighter 9 5 east

# Of two held the latest pressed wins; letting it go falls back to the other.
cmd walk 4 2
wait 2
trace leaderpos,walkstep,held
hold Down 8
hold Down+Left 24
expect state held south west
hold Down 30
trace off
wait 20
expect actor fighter 2 4 south

# A wall only turns the leader.
cmd walk 1 5
wait 2
press Left
wait 20
expect actor fighter 1 5 west
expect state walkstep -

# Run held: half the step time at the layouts' 200%; RunToggle keeps it running without Run, and toggles back.
cmd walk 2 5
wait 2
hold LeftShift+Right 2
expect state walkstep east 3 5 32/125 200%
hold LeftShift+Right 58
wait 20
expect actor fighter 10 5 east
press CapsLock
expect state always_run on
cmd walk 2 5
wait 2
hold Right 2
expect statehas walkstep /125 200%
hold Right 10
hold LeftShift+Right 2
wait 40
expect statehas walkstep -
press CapsLock
expect state always_run off

# The D-pad, and the stick through its hysteresis: near the diagonal it keeps the axis it took first, and inside the
# smaller dead zone it lets go.
cmd walk 3 4
wait 2
padhold DPadRight 10
expect state held east
wait 20
padstick 0.7 0.6 4
expect state held east
padstick 0.6 0.7 4
expect state held east
padstick 0.3 0.3 2
expect state held -
padstick 0.2 0.9 2
expect state held north
wait 30

# Aboard the skimmer a step takes the vehicle's own time.
dev vehicle reed_skimmer brine_flats mooring
dev warp brine_flats 6 4
wait 4
press Right
wait 2
press Enter
wait 2
expect state aboard reed_skimmer
hold Right 2
expect statehas walkstep east 8 4 32/125 100%
hold Right 30
wait 20
expect state vehicles reed_skimmer 11 4 aboard
shot build/shots/field/walk_aboard.png
press Enter
wait 4

# A belt carries the party a cell per its own 200 ms, which the glide takes too.
dev warp cogway 2 3
trace leaderpos,walkstep
wait 30
trace off
expect statehas walkstep carried
wait 40
expect state leader cogway 6 3

# The classic field: a tap only turns the leader, a hold walks as before.
fieldinput classic
expect state fieldinput classic
dev warp tidewater start
wait 10
press Right
wait 20
expect actor fighter 3 4 east
hold Down 20
wait 20
expect actor fighter 3 5 south
