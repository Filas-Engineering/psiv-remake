# The data copy scripts/make_rules_data.py writes (Wren Cellar with game.json's interact nearby, run_speed_percent 200,
# arrive_notice walked, step_timing exact at 133.5 ms a cell, a counter drawn as '=', music by the door's flag and for the
# skimmer's battles) and its asset copy (an object over both cells of the long table, a title that lists Erase), with
# --dev and the real-time field input. With --window the shot lines save screenshots under build/shots/rules/ (run from
# bindings/).

cmd set text instant
expect screen Title
wait 1
expect state selected New game
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 400
fieldinput realtime
# arrive_notice walked: the new game's start announces nothing.
expect state banner -
expect notoast You arrive
dev warp wren_cellar cellar_door
dev encounters off
wait 2
expect state area Wren Cellar
expect state banner -
expect state music field_marsh_reeds
shot build/shots/rules/cellar.png

# The closed exit below the door is drawn as its normal tile with the door over it, and blocks.
expect state fieldpicture -
cmd walk 6 8
cmd face south
wait 2
hold Down 20
expect actor fighter 6 8 south

# The prompt follows the session's target: the clerk across the counter drawn as '='.
cmd walk 2 3
cmd face north
wait 2
expect state interacttarget read at 2 1 facing north across
expect state interactprompt read at 2 1
shot build/shots/rules/prompt_across.png
expect state interactat.2.1 read at 2 1 facing north across

# The long table answers from below either of its cells, under the object drawn over both.
cmd walk 9 3
cmd face north
wait 2
expect state interacttarget read at 9 2 facing north
expect statehas objects 2x1
shot build/shots/rules/table_object.png

# The cook stands on its actor_at cell behind its counter, and answers there from beside it.
expect actor cellar_cook 10 5
cmd walk 9 5
cmd face west
wait 2
expect state interacttarget talk at 10 5 facing east
expect state interactprompt talk at 10 5

# Running through the session: the drawn step is the session's run step time, and play time moves with the frames only.
cmd walk 2 6
cmd face east
wait 2
trace walkstep,playtime,leaderpos
hold LeftShift+Right 3
expect statehas walkstep 200%
hold LeftShift+Right 17
trace off
wait 10

# The notice opens the door; the area's music follows its flag once the event is over.
cmd walk 5 3
cmd face north
wait 2
expect state interacttarget read at 5 2 facing north
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 40
wait 4
expect state music title_harbor_dawn

# Walking out through the exit announces the hollow (banner and notice).
cmd walk 6 8
cmd face south
wait 2
hold Down 20
wait 2
expect state area Wren Hollow
expect state banner Wren Hollow
expect toast You arrive at Wren Hollow.
shot build/shots/rules/arrival_banner.png

# Wren Hollow's actors from the library's state: the tallykeeper on its actor_at cell behind the counter, the sentinel in
# an enemy's figure; the scene places the drifter, fades it in, walks it along its routes, hops it, dissolves it and
# removes it, and the keeper's own `tally_nod` lasts the 0.7 seconds game.json gives it as an object.
expect actor tallykeeper 4 1
expect state actorlook.hollow_sentinel enemy aegis_sentinel
expect actor hollow_sentinel 7 4 west
cmd walk 4 3
cmd face north
wait 2
expect state interacttarget talk at 4 2 facing north
press Enter
expect screen Dialogue
press Enter
waitstatehas 40 actorlook.hollow_drifter fade_in
expect pose hollow_drifter fadein
expect actor tallykeeper 4 1 south
shot build/shots/rules/drifter_fade_in.png
waitstate 60 actorlook.hollow_drifter own
expect actor hollow_drifter 1 4 east
press Enter
# The pathfinding move walks the record's route round the leader and the sentinel, not along x then y.
waitstate 20 actorroute.hollow_drifter 1,4 1,3 2,3 3,3 4,3 5,3 6,3 7,3 8,3 8,4
wait 30
expect actor hollow_drifter walking
shot build/shots/rules/drifter_route.png
waitstatehas 200 actorlook.hollow_drifter hop
expect pose hollow_drifter jump
waitstate 60 actorroute.hollow_drifter 8,4 7,4 6,4 5,4 4,4 3,4 2,4 2,3 1,3 1,4
waitstatehas 120 actorlook.hollow_drifter dissolve
wait 20
# Hidden by the library at once, it is drawn dissolving for the animation's 0.8 seconds.
expect pose hollow_drifter dissolve
shot build/shots/rules/drifter_dissolve.png
waitstate 60 actorlook.hollow_drifter own hidden
expect actor hollow_drifter hidden
press Enter
waitstatehas 10 actorlook.tallykeeper tally_nod
expect statehas actorlook.tallykeeper /700
expect state actorlook.hollow_drifter own removed
waitstatehas 60 actorlook.tallykeeper shake
pressuntil Enter Field 200
expect actor hollow_drifter hidden

# The second talk fades the keeper out and removes it for good, then reloads the area.
cmd face north
wait 2
press Enter
press Enter
waitstatehas 120 actorlook.tallykeeper fade_out
expect pose tallykeeper fadeout
pressuntil Enter Field 200
wait 2
expect actor tallykeeper hidden
expect state actorlook.tallykeeper own removed
