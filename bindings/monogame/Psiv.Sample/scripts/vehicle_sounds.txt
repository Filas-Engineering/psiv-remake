# A vehicle's move sound once per step aboard (make_vehicle_sound_data.py's copy, `move_ms` 0, with --dev): a scripted
# path walk takes its seven steps in one update, and the seven sounds play one a step's time apart, not on one frame; a
# click-walk path and the real-time input play one for each step they take.

cmd set text instant
expect screen Title
press Enter
pressuntil Enter Field 800
dev encounters off
dev vehicle reed_skimmer reed_path skimmer_front
cmd walk 13 4
cmd walk 9 6
cmd walk 16 6
press Right
wait 3
press Enter
wait 4
expect state aboard reed_skimmer
expect state vehicles reed_skimmer 17 6 aboard

# A scripted walk: seven steps at once.
audio mark
cmd walk 10 6
wait 1
expect state vehicles reed_skimmer 10 6 aboard
expect audiocount 1 cue lookout_creak
wait 6
expect audiocount 1 cue lookout_creak
wait 2
expect audiocount 2 cue lookout_creak
wait 60
expect audiocount 7 cue lookout_creak

# A click-walk path: five steps, five sounds.
audio mark
clickcell 15 6
wait 90
expect state vehicles reed_skimmer 15 6 aboard
expect audiocount 5 cue lookout_creak

# The real-time input: three steps west, three sounds.
fieldinput realtime
audio mark
hold Left 22
wait 30
expect state vehicles reed_skimmer 12 6 aboard
expect audiocount 3 cue lookout_creak
