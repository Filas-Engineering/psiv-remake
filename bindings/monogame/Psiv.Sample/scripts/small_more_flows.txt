# The second layout flows at 320x224 (make_more_flows_assets.py --small, the small set) on the more flows data copy, from
# saves of the scripted playthrough, with --dev: the intro's scrolling strip and cropped layer at a quarter of the size,
# the PNG's half on the menu, the Load screen's slot blocks in a grid two wide (four rows a page, sprites and levels in
# each block, the empty slot's own template), the inn's decline and farewell lines, the shop's question before the YES/NO,
# and the level-ups two lines a page with the page arrow. With --window the shot lines save screenshots under
# build/shots/small/ (run from bindings/).

cmd set text instant
expect screen Title
waitstate 60 uifield.title.intro_step story
expect statehas pixel.130.85 dc3c3c
expect statehas pixel.165.85 3cc85a
waitstatehas 50 pixel.130.85 f0d23c
expect statehas pixel.165.85 dc3c3c
shot build/shots/small/more_intro_story.png
waitstate 40 uifield.title.intro_step logo
expect statehas pixel.146.110 9646c8
shot build/shots/small/more_intro_logo.png
press Enter
wait 3
press Enter
wait 3
expect state uifield.title.start false
expect statehas pixel.6.10 f08c28
shot build/shots/small/more_title_menu.png
